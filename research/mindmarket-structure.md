# MindMarket Homepage — Structure Teardown

Captured from the live site on 2026-09-10 at desktop (1440×900) and mobile (390×844) widths.

## Page anatomy

1. Floating white navigation shell with separate CTA treatment.
2. Full-screen green hero with oversized two-line headline, centered subhead and a Rive ensemble entering from below.
3. Cream rounded story surface overlapping the hero.
4. Introductory statement and CTA.
5. Approximately 7,400px illustrated timeline containing four content cards, multiple Rive characters, small floating SVG props, and one continuous thick green path.
6. Layered character finale at the end of the timeline.
7. Large rounded green callout headed “Ready when you are”.
8. Three oversized number cards.
9. Horizontal editorial cards.
10. Three continuously moving brand rails.
11. Oversized yellow contact footer.

## Measured reference values

- Desktop hero heading: 139.68px / 132.69px line height.
- Desktop navigation: 1224px wide, 68px high.
- Main timeline path viewport: `1944.2 × 6151.5`.
